Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

AutoFlow is a university group project. This website is a marketing page for a product that is still in development. This policy explains what we do with the personal information you share with us here.

1. Who we are

AutoFlow is a student project team based in Sri Lanka. You can reach us at hello@autoflow.lk.

2. What information we collect

  • Waitlist form: your name, email address, role (for example vehicle owner or service center owner), optional mobile number, and your consent.
  • Contact form: your name, email address, optional subject and your message.
  • Usage analytics: if analytics is enabled, anonymous information such as pages viewed, approximate location (country/city), device and browser type. IP addresses are anonymised.

3. How we use your information

  • To add you to the early-access waitlist and email you about AutoFlow's launch and pilot.
  • To reply to your questions and feedback.
  • To understand how many people visit the site and register, so we can improve it.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Legal basis and consent

We process your waitlist and contact details only with your consent, given by ticking the consent box before you submit a form. You may withdraw consent at any time by emailing us.

5. Who can see your information

Only members of the AutoFlow project team and, for academic assessment, our university supervisors can access submissions. Submissions are processed by the form-handling provider and the hosting provider we use to run this website, which act on our instructions. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. How we protect it

This site is served over HTTPS. Form data is sent over an encrypted connection and stored with a reputable provider with restricted, password-protected access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

7. How long we keep it

Waitlist and contact details are kept until the project concludes or until you ask us to delete them, whichever is sooner. Analytics data is kept in aggregated form.

8. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email hello@autoflow.lk and we will respond promptly. Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022, gives individuals rights over their personal data; we aim to respect the principles of consent, purpose limitation, retention limits and deletion.

9. Cookies and analytics

The site itself does not set marketing cookies. If analytics is enabled, the analytics provider (for example Google Analytics or Plausible) may set or use cookies or similar technologies to count visits and registrations.

10. Children

This site is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the project evolves. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@autoflow.lk.
