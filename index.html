The problem

Vehicle service is still run on paper, phone calls and guesswork

Most service centers in Sri Lanka are small and busy. Without a shared system, everyone – from the vehicle owner to the center owner – pays the price.

Vehicle owners

"Is my vehicle ready yet?"

Customers wait, call and wait again. There is no live status, no queue position and no idea how long the job will really take.

Vehicle owners

Surprise extra charges

Extra faults are found mid-job and added to the bill by phone – with no photo, no written consent and plenty of room for disputes.

Staff & managers

Manual job hand-offs

Someone has to shout, write or walk a job card from washing to scanning to repair. Vehicles sit idle while staff wait to be told what is next.

Managers

No view of the floor

Delays, overloaded technicians and unanswered approvals are noticed only when a customer complains.

Vehicle owners

Lost papers, missed expiries

Revenue licence, insurance and emission test papers live in glove boxes. Renewal dates are forgotten and service history disappears.

Center owners

Numbers in notebooks

Revenue, job times and staff performance are scattered across books and chat threads, so growth decisions are made on instinct.

One platform. Four apps. A workflow that runs itself.

AutoFlow gives every person in the service journey their own app, all powered by a shared backend and a job workflow engine that moves each vehicle through wash, scan and repair automatically.

How it works

From booking to "ready for pickup" in seven steps

The engine does the hand-offs. People do the work.

BookCheck-inSplitWorkHand-offNextReady
  1. 01

    Book

    From the app, by phone or at the counter. The system issues a booking token and reserves the slot.

  2. 02

    Check in

    Reception scans the token, QR or number plate, takes entry photos and opens the job card.

  3. 03

    Stage split

    Chosen services become ordered stages – for example Waterwash, then Scanning, then Engine repair.

  4. 04

    Stage work

    The least-loaded available staff member in that category gets the job, with a step checklist and timer.

  5. 05

    Stage close

    Closing a stage moves the vehicle to the next category's queue, and the customer sees the new status.

  6. 06

    Next vehicle

    The moment staff close a job, the next vehicle in the slot queue is assigned to them.

  7. 07

    Final close

    Extras are listed, the manager signs off quality, the invoice is generated and the customer is told the vehicle is ready.
